- Lower latency and smoother output:
  - every captured frame is rendered immediately instead of waiting for the next display refresh, and no new frame is skipped while the previous one is still rendering
  - no fixed 60 fps grid any more (it could drop frames through timing jitter); on 120 Hz (ProMotion) displays MacGlass captures and renders at 120 fps
  - smaller capture and output queues (fewer buffered frames)
  - animated shaders keep their speed at any frame rate
