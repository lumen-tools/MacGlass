- Screen Recording: new **Reset Permission** button in the hint. If MacGlass is switched on in System Settings but still cannot record (the entry belongs to an older, differently signed MacGlass), one click removes the old entry and macOS asks again – allow it and choose "Quit & Reopen".
- The hint shows macOS' own reason when recording is refused.
- At launch, MacGlass asks macOS once (as in 0.1.0), never repeatedly. Menus stay usable while access is missing.
